How to order

No cart. Just a DM.

Every order goes through Instagram, so you talk to the people who made the piece.

  1. Pick your pieces

    Choose a size and add each piece to the order slip.

  2. Copy the message and DM @blqruby

    The slip writes the message for you. Paste it into Instagram.

  3. We reply in the thread

    You get price, availability and how to pick up or ship.
